product

## Users

My Beach App serves adults, locals, and vacationers near the Loire-Atlantique coast who want to discover, create, and join nearby Beach Activities with minimal planning. They may be outside, on mobile, and trying to quickly understand what is happening around them, where to meet, and whether there are still Spots available.

## Product Purpose

My Beach App helps Visitors and Signed-in Users find, create, and join geolocated Beach Activities in a few minutes. The MVP succeeds when a user can see nearby activities, create a Beach Activity with a precise Meeting Point, and join an open activity in one tap.

## Brand Personality

Bright, coastal, lightweight.

The product should feel spontaneous, premium, readable outdoors, and simple enough to use while already on the beach. Customer-facing copy is short, direct, warm French using `tu`.

## Anti-references

This should not look or behave like a social network, marketplace, enterprise dashboard, dark admin product, generic template, chat app, or feed-first community product. Avoid neon styling, over-glassed surfaces, platform-fragmented native experiments, decorative effects, and any MVP drift toward chat, groups, payments, reputation, social feed, or marketplace features.

## Design Principles

1. Map-first clarity: the map and Meeting Point interactions should stay central, compact, and immediately understandable.
2. Fast outdoor decisions: hierarchy, contrast, and touch targets should work in bright mobile contexts.
3. Create in minutes: creation flows should stay stepped, narrow, and focused on the next required decision.
4. Familiar product controls: use standard mobile patterns and the Tamagui-based design system before inventing new affordances.
5. Narrow MVP discipline: every screen should support seeing, creating, or joining Beach Activities without adding social-product scope.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable body text at 16 px or larger, touch targets of at least 44 px, visible labels for inputs, and state indicators that do not rely on color alone. Preserve reduced-motion expectations, keep interfaces coherent on iOS and Android, and maintain strong contrast for outdoor readability.
